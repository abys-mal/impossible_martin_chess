Read the unfair fine print
  • Your king is frozen. Block or capture to escape check.
  • Pawns advance one square and never promote.
  • Rooks, bishops and queens cannot land on edges.
  • Every non-knight piece rests for your next turn.
  • Knights ignore fatigue and rest, but go astray 10% of the time.
  • Martin may refuse an unprotected capture (8%). A refused move stays blocked this turn. Your only legal move is protected from refusal.
  • Martin promotes a rank early and can take any piece that moves beside his pawn by reverse en passant.
  • Queue one premove during Martin's turn. If it works, a 15-second penalty is guaranteed.
